🌸 豐田村

Fengtian Village · 彰化縣田尾鄉 Tianwei Township, Changhua County

第二選區

縣府旅遊網把豐田列為公路花園一帶的村之一，以花卉聞名。田尾鄉是全國最大的康乃馨產區。

The county tourism site lists Fengtian among the villages of the Highway Garden area, known for flowers. Tianwei is the largest carnation-growing area in Taiwan.
